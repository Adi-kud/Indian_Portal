National Flag

The National Flag is a horizontal tricolour of India saffron (kesria) at the top, white in the middle and green at the bottom in equal proportion.

National Anthem

The song Jana-Gana-Mana, composed originally in Bangla by Rabindranath Tagore was adopted in its Hindi version by the Constituent Assembly as the National Anthem of India on January 24, 1950.

National Song

The song Vande Matram, composed in Sanskrit by Bankimchandra Chatterji, was a source of inspiration to the people in their struggle for freedom. It has an equal status with Jana-Gana-Mana.

National Bird

The Indian peacock, the national bird of India, is a colourful, swan-sized bird, with a fan-shaped crest of feathers, a white patch under the eye and a long, slender neck.
